Common questions

The pet isn't appearing on my screen.
Pocket Shimeji needs the "Display over other apps" permission to show your pet outside the app. Open Settings → Apps → Pocket Shimeji → Display over other apps, and make sure it's turned on. See our Privacy Policy for why the App needs this permission.

I'm not getting my daily streak reminder.
Reminders use Android notifications, which require the notification permission on Android 13+. Check Settings → Apps → Pocket Shimeji → Notifications is enabled, and that the in-app streak reminder toggle (Settings → Notifications) is on.

How do I delete a custom pet or reset the app?
See our Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

Does Pocket Shimeji show ads or require a login?
No. The App has no ads, no account system, and does not require sign-in.
