Data Deletion

Pocket Shimeji: Screen Pet

Effective date: August 6, 2026 · Developer: BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD

App data for Pocket Shimeji (settings, streak, unlocked pets, and custom pets) is stored on your device. Use the steps below to delete it.

Delete a single custom pet

  1. Open Pocket Shimeji.
  2. Go to your pet Collection.
  3. Select the custom pet you created.
  4. Choose Delete, and confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes that pet's images and data from your device.

Delete everything the App has stored

(settings, streak, unlocked pets, custom pets)

Option A — Clear app storage (keeps the App installed):

  1. On your Android device, open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications) → Pocket Shimeji.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data), and confirm.

This deletes all locally stored preferences, streak/score progress, unlocked-pet state, and any custom pets you created. The App will start fresh the next time you open it, as if newly installed.

Option B — Uninstall the App:

  1. Long-press the Pocket Shimeji icon, or go to Settings → Apps → Pocket Shimeji.
  2. Choose Uninstall, and confirm.

Uninstalling removes the App and all of its locally stored data from your device.
