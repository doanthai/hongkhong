Privacy Policy

Pocket Shimeji: Screen Pet

Effective date: August 6, 2026 · Developer: BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Shimeji: Screen Pet ("Pocket Shimeji," "the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the app. Pocket Shimeji is developed by BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD.

1. Information we process

Pocket Shimeji does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or other profile identifiers to us. Gameplay and preference data you create in the app (pets, streak, settings, custom drawings) is stored in the app’s private storage on your device.

Separately, the App uses Google services that are standard in our Android builds — including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, and where configured Remote Config). Those services may process device and advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and similar technical data as described in Google’s policies, so that we can show ads, measure stability, and configure the product. We do not sell your personal information.

We do not ask for location, contacts, phone, camera, or microphone permissions for Pocket Shimeji’s core pet features. Custom-pet images you pick are handled on-device (system Photo Picker + local crop) and are not uploaded to BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD servers as pet artwork.

2. Information stored locally on your device

  • App settings (pet animation speed, pet size, notification toggle, and similar preferences)
  • Score and daily streak count
  • Which pets in the catalog you have unlocked
  • Custom pets you create (including imported/cropped images and resulting frames)

This local gameplay data is not copied to a BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD account backend. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that on-device data. Clearing storage does not by itself erase data that Google’s ad or analytics SDKs may retain under their own retention rules.

4. Third-party services

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The App is built to use Google Mobile Ads to show advertisements. Google may collect or receive information such as advertising ID, device and app information, and interaction data. See Google’s advertising policies and this app’s Play Data safety form.

Firebase (Google)

The App includes Firebase components typical of our releases — including Analytics, Crashlytics, and (where configured) Remote Config — under Google’s Firebase privacy terms.

Other libraries

uCrop is used only for on-device image cropping when you import a photo for a custom pet.

5. Children's privacy

Pocket Shimeji is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. Parents who have concerns can remove on-device app data (Section 6) and review Google’s ad/personalization settings on the device.

6. Data deletion

There is no BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD user account to delete. Remove a custom pet in Collection, or clear app storage / uninstall via Android Settings. See our Data Deletion page. For advertising or analytics data held by Google, use Google’s device/account controls as well.

7. Security

On-device data is protected by Android’s per-app sandbox. Data processed by Google ads and Firebase is handled under Google’s practices for those products.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how third-party SDKs are used, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
