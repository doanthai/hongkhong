Privacy Policy

Pocket Shimeji: Screen Pet

Effective date: August 3, 2026 · Developer: BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Shimeji: Screen Pet ("Pocket Shimeji," "the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the app.

Short version: Pocket Shimeji does not have an account system, does not use ads, does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not send your data to a server. Everything the app remembers about you — your pet collection, streak, settings, and any pet you draw or customize — stays stored on your device only.

1. Information we do — and do not — collect

We do not operate a backend server for this app, and the app does not currently make any network requests. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information with us or with any third party.

Specifically, the app does not:

  • ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or any identifying information
  • collect location, contacts, phone, camera, or microphone data (the app never requests these permissions)
  • include any advertising SDK
  • include any analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • transmit your custom pet drawings/imported images anywhere

2. Information stored locally on your device

To provide its core features, Pocket Shimeji stores the following only on your device, using Android's private app storage:

  • Your app settings (pet animation speed, pet size, notification toggle, and similar preferences)
  • Your score and daily streak count
  • Which pets in the catalog you have unlocked
  • Any custom pet you create — including the image you pick to draw or trace a skin, and the resulting pet frames

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD server. If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Android Settings, this data is permanently deleted.

3. Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy the app asks for it
Display over other appsThis is the core feature of Pocket Shimeji: it lets your pet walk, climb, and sit on top of your screen while you use other apps. Without this permission, the pet cannot appear outside the Pocket Shimeji app itself.
Run in the foreground / "special use" foreground serviceKeeps the pet-overlay service alive in the background so your pet keeps moving around your screen instead of disappearing the moment you switch apps.
NotificationsUsed for (a) a persistent notification while your pet overlay is active, and (b) an optional daily reminder about your streak. You can decline this — the pet and the rest of the app still work, you simply won't get the reminder or persistent notice.
Receive boot completedLets the app automatically bring your pet back after you restart your phone, if the pet overlay was turned on before the restart.
InternetReserved for a possible future feature (downloading additional pet characters). As of this version, the app makes no network calls at all — this permission is currently unused. If that changes in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy first.

We never ask for storage/photos permission directly: when you choose a photo to draw or crop a custom pet, the app opens the standard Android system Photo Picker, which lets you pick one image without granting the app broader access to your photo library. That image is cropped and processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

4. Third-party services

The only third-party software component bundled with the app is uCrop, an open-source, on-device image cropping library used solely when you choose to import a photo for a custom pet. It performs its work locally on your device and does not communicate with any server. We do not integrate any advertising network, analytics platform, or crash-reporting service in this app.

5. Children's privacy

Pocket Shimeji is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information from any user.

6. Data deletion

Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is no "delete my account" request to make — there is no account. See our Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions to remove data from your device.

7. Security

Your data is protected by Android's standard per-app sandboxing, which keeps other apps from reading Pocket Shimeji's private storage. Since no data is transmitted off your device, there is no server-side transmission to secure.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how it behaves, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
