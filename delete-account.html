Account Management

Delete Your Account

Permanently delete your account and all associated data from any BBL Technology app.

⚠️

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including your profile, usage history, AI-generated content, and subscription information will be permanently deleted. Active subscriptions will not be automatically refunded — please cancel your subscription before deleting your account.

Submit a Deletion Request

Fill in the form below and we will process your account deletion within 30 days.

Or Delete Directly From the App

1

Open the App & Sign In

Open the BBL Technology app on your device and make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.

2

Go to Profile / Settings

Tap the Profile or Settings icon — usually in the bottom navigation bar or top-right corner of the app.

3

Find "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Settings or Profile screen to find the "Delete Account" option.

4

Confirm Deletion

Tap "Delete Account" and confirm the prompt. You may be asked to re-enter your password for security.

What Happens After Deletion?

  • Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • Some data may be retained for up to 7 years where required by Hong Kong law (e.g., financial transaction records).
  • AI-generated content and your usage history will be permanently removed.
  • You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete.
  • You may re-register after deletion, but your previous data cannot be restored.

Cancel Your Subscription First

Before deleting your account, cancel any active subscriptions to avoid future charges:

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Select the app → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play Store → Menu (☰) → Subscriptions → Select the app → Cancel Subscription
